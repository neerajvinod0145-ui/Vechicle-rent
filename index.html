About the project

Smarter shared mobility

VahanaShare is a student project prototype that demonstrates vehicle discovery and basic booking management through a responsive website.

How it works

  1. Browse the available bikes and cars.
  2. Choose a vehicle and enter your name and rental dates.
  3. Confirm the booking and view it under My Bookings.
  4. Cancel a booking when you no longer need it.

Demo note: no real payment or identity verification is performed.
